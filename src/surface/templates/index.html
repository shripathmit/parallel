How it works

From FDA publication to stakeholder prediction

Four automated steps turn a raw regulatory change into behavior-pattern clone predictions.

01
Sense

Monitors watch FDA sources around the clock. Detections arrive via webhook and enter the pipeline as raw events.

02
Understand

The Task API extracts what changed, scores severity, and grounds every claim in the source document.

03
Clone

Behavior patterns are matched deterministically — trigger coverage × confidence — to each stakeholder clone.

04
Simulate

Enforcement, reviewer, and sponsor clones predict what happens next — with citations and patterns fired.
