How clones are built

1. Mine — FindAll scans FDA records and the Task API distills recurring behavior patterns per stakeholder. 2. Match — a deterministic matcher scores every pattern by trigger coverage × confidence and hands the top matches to that stakeholder's clone. 3. Reason — the clone predicts from the analysis plus its matched patterns, and cites which fired.
